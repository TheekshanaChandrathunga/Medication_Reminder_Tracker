3. Key Development Activities
3.1 Medication Search
Completed medication search so users can search by name, view matching medications, and receive a suitable message when no matches are found.
3.2 Medication Photo Upload
Completed the medication photo feature, including selecting or capturing a photo, associating it with the correct medication, displaying it with medication details, and handling upload errors.
3.3 Daily Medication Summary
Completed the daily summary to show medications scheduled for a selected day, taken/missed/remaining doses, and recorded dose-status updates.
3.4 Medication Notes
Completed the ability to add, edit, and delete notes associated with the correct medication.
3.5 CSV Report Export
Completed CSV export for supported medication and adherence data, including saving or downloading the file and providing feedback when export fails.
3.6 Caregiver Weekly Reports
Completed caregiver access to weekly medication and adherence information for authorized patients, including taken and missed dose details.
3.7 Caregiver Management of Multiple Patients
Completed the ability for caregivers to view their authorized patient list, select a patient, view that patient's schedule and adherence information, keep patient records separated, and prevent unauthorized access.
