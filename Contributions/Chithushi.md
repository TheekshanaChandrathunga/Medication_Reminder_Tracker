## My Role

I worked as a **UI/UX designer and frontend developer** for the Medication Reminder & Tracker project. My main responsibility was designing the complete application UI in Figma and developing the patient-side frontend.

## What I Did

- Designed the complete application user interface and user flows in Figma for both patients and caregivers.
- Created the Figma designs for the patient and caregiver dashboards, medication management, history, reports, profile, and settings screens.
- Developed the patient-side frontend using Flutter.
- Implemented and styled the patient screens, including:
  - Splash, registration, and login screens
  - Home dashboard
  - Medication list and add medication screens
  - Medication details and history
  - Reports, profile, and settings
- Connected the screens with the application's navigation and authentication flow.
- Added user-friendly form validation, loading indicators, password visibility controls, and error messages.
- Focused on readable layouts, consistent colors and spacing, simple navigation, and touch-friendly controls.

## Challenges I Faced

- Converting the Figma designs into responsive Flutter layouts.
- Keeping both patient and caregiver interfaces simple, clear, and accessible.
- Maintaining a consistent design across all patient screens.
- Managing form validation, loading states, error handling, and navigation.
- Matching the Flutter implementation with the original Figma designs while working with the existing project structure.

## Outcome

My contribution provided the complete Figma UI design for both patients and caregivers, along with the patient-facing Flutter frontend. These designs created a clear and accessible experience for managing medications and supporting patients.
