## 2. Project Overview

The Medication Reminder & Tracker is a software application designed to help users manage their medications, schedule reminders, track medication adherence, and generate reports. The system also supports caregiver functionality, allowing caregivers to assist patients with medication management and monitor their progress.

The project was developed using the Agile Scrum methodology, with development work divided into five sprints. The team organized requirements into user stories, estimated effort using story points, and monitored progress through the product backlog and sprint backlogs.
